## Legal/compliance caution

Current IRS public guidance says a Section 501(c)(19) veterans organization generally needs at least 75% past or present Armed Forces members and at least 97.5% within the permitted service/cadet/family categories. A 2026 IRS determination letter still applied Treasury Regulation 1.501(c)(19)-1 as requiring 75% war veterans. The organization should therefore track both tests conservatively until the IRS determination or qualified tax counsel resolves the issue for YAT-YAS. Donor deductibility has a separate 90% war-veteran rule.

Do not weaken the membership-composition safeguards without documenting the legal basis.
