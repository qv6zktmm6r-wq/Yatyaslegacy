Mission

The tracks motto

The motto You ain’t tracks, you ain’t shit.

YAT-YAS is the amphibious tracks community motto — the full call shared among Alligator Marines who manned the iron from ship to shore: You ain’t tracks, you ain’t shit.

It belongs to the Assault Amphibian lineage: 2d Tracks at Camp Lejeune, 3d Tracks at Camp Pendleton, and 4th Tracks of Marine Forces Reserve — across LVT, LVTP, AAV, and today’s ACV.

YAT-YAS Association is being built as the permanent network and historical record of that community — useful while you’re still serving, built across generations, digital-first, and with a defined place for family.

What it means

You ain’t tracks, you ain’t shit. — the Assault Amphibian community motto, carried by 2d, 3d, and 4th Tracks across the LVT-to-ACV lineage.

Priorities

Built for every generation

Brotherhood, legacy, the next mission, and family — the four priorities that keep the tracks community connected.

    Brotherhood

    Membership, Crew Finder, regional gatherings, chapters, the YAT-YAS Muster, and year-round fellowship.

  • II

    Legacy

  • III

    Next Mission

    Spouses, descendants, and families through events, heritage projects, scholarships, volunteer work, and mutual support.

Legacy

The iron lineage

One community across generations of amphibious vehicles — from Alligator to ACV.

  1. 1940s

    LVT

    Landing Vehicle Tracked

    The Alligator that carried Marines from ship to shore across coral and surf — the beginning of the lineage.

  2. Postwar

    LVTP

    Landing Vehicle, Tracked, Personnel

    Personnel carriers that refined amphibious assault doctrine for a new generation of operations.

  3. Modern era

    AAV

    Assault Amphibious Vehicle

    The workhorse of late twentieth- and early twenty-first-century ship-to-shore movement.

  4. Today

    ACVP

    Amphibious Combat Vehicle — Personnel

    The personnel carrier of the modern lineage — 8×8 wheeled, troop-carrying, and still writing its story.

The First Wave

The first 100 approved qualifying service members get a permanent place on the roll — FW-001 through FW-100 — before anyone else.

  • FW

    Your number, forever

    A permanent First Wave designation on the Association roll — recognition that stays with you, not a seat for sale.

  • II

    Welcome package

    Your $100 entrance goes toward the welcome membership package — numbered coin, box, t-shirt, card materials, and Hub access when produced.

  • III

    Only 100

    When the roll fills, it closes. First Wave does not transfer and does not make anyone a founder or officer.

Association annual award

Carry the lineage forward through education

National scholarships and trade-school awards for dependents of Alligator Marines connected to the LVT–ACV lineage — when the tracks connection is verified.

Funds cover documented education costs — tuition, fees, books, required tools or training materials — paid to the school when practical, or reimbursed against receipts.

Who can apply

Eligibility

  • Child, stepchild, adopted child, or dependent of a tracks community sponsor
  • Sponsor tied to the amphibious-vehicle community (LVT through ACV)
  • Enrolled in or accepted to an eligible education or trade program
  • Able to verify relationship, tracks connection, and enrollment
