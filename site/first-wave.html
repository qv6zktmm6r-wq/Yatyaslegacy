The First Wave

FW-001 through FW-100 — the first 100 approved qualifying service members. Historical recognition only.

Service members only

First Wave seats are for qualifying past or present U.S. Armed Forces members connected to the amphibious tracks community. Spouses, descendants, and supporters join through Legacy and Friend paths — not FW-001–100.

$100 entrance — applied to your welcome membership package: numbered coin, presentation box, t-shirt, membership card materials, and Member Hub access. That hundred dollars is for your package — not a vague “dues dump.”

  • Permanent FW number when approved (FW-001–100)
  • Welcome package funds reserved for your kit (ships when produced)
  • Not ownership, not a Board seat, not transferable
  • Ongoing membership: $5 / month — supports scholarships, musters & events, and Association programs

Join the waitlist so the Board can verify service connection. When Checkout is live, pay entrance on the Member Hub — your FW number is issued only after payment clears. Welcome kit ships when produced.

YAT-YAS Association, Inc.

YAT-YAS Association preserves and perpetuates the history, traditions, fellowship, welfare, and legacy of past and present members of the United States Armed Forces associated with the amphibious-vehicle community.
