The Roll

Remembered with family blessing

The first remembrances will be placed here with their families’ permission. Until then, the Roll stands ready — quiet, permanent, and waiting.

How it works

Dignity first

  1. 01

    Family asks

    A family member or someone they trust sends a remembrance request.

  2. 02

    Board verifies

    We contact the family before anything is prepared for the public Roll.

  3. 03

    Family approves

    The final text is theirs. Nothing publishes until they say yes.

  4. 04

    Published

    The remembrance joins the Roll. Family may edit or withdraw at any time.

  • Nothing is public without recorded family approval.
  • We never invent service, medals, or combat actions.
  • Family can withdraw a remembrance at any time.
  • Prefer to talk first? Email board@yatyasassociation.com.
