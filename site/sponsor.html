Sponsor or partner with us

Companies, foundations, and community partners — tell the Board how you want to stand with the tracks community. Every sponsor is invited to our annual muster.

We send you the form — you tell us the intent

Personal gifts go through Donate. Organization sponsorships and partnerships start here. The Board reviews every inquiry, then follows up with next steps (invoice, agreement, or custom payment link). After approval, your logo joins the public Sponsors wall, and you are invited to the Association’s annual muster.

  • Invitation to the annual muster for every sponsor
  • Logo on the Association Sponsors wall after Board approval
  • Scholarships and trade awards for tracks-community dependents
  • Gatherings, musters, and fellowship events
  • Legacy programs — oral history, memorials, heritage record

YAT-YAS Association is an independent California nonprofit corporation. Sponsorship does not imply USMC or DoD endorsement.
