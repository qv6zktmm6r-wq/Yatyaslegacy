# YAT-YAS Association — Strategic Blueprint

Status: Working strategy. This document describes the operating vision for the Association. It is not a substitute for the Articles, bylaws, board resolutions, tax filings, or legal advice.

## Core idea

YAT-YAS Association should be more than a dues-based veterans club or reunion organization. The long-term goal is to become the living professional, historical, family, and fellowship network for the amphibious-vehicle community across generations:

**LVT → LVTP → AAV → ACV**

The machines changed. The brotherhood did not.

## Public positioning

**YAT-YAS Association**  
**One Brotherhood. Every Generation.**

Supporting line:

> Preserving the people, history, traditions, and future of the Amtracker community.

The Association should deliberately connect active-duty Marines, retirees, veterans, spouses, descendants, historians, and supporters without implying Department of Defense or Marine Corps endorsement.

## Four operating pillars

### 1. Brotherhood

Purpose: keep the community connected while members are serving, after transition, and across generations.

Primary initiatives:

- verified member network
- Crew Finder
- YAT-YAS Muster
- regional Breakfast Clubs
- chapters
- member events
- volunteer opportunities
- digital membership card
- member voting and governance tools

### 2. Legacy

Purpose: preserve the history before stories, photographs, artifacts, and first-hand knowledge are lost.

Primary initiatives:

- YAT-YAS Legacy Registry
- Amtracker Oral History Project
- Final Splash memorial
- Heritage Map
- museum partnerships
- vehicle-restoration support
- photographic and document archives
- unit and platform history
- Founding 100 permanent record

### 3. Next Mission

Purpose: make YAT-YAS valuable to Marines before and after they leave active service.

Primary initiatives:

- veteran-to-active-duty mentorship
- transition mentorship
- career networking
- education and trade-school guidance
- entrepreneurship mentorship
- professional introductions
- leadership mentorship
- transition resource library

Working program name:

**Track to Next Mission**

### 4. Family

Purpose: recognize that the Amtracker community extends beyond the individual Marine.

Primary initiatives:

- spouse and family participation
- Legacy Member programming
- family events
- scholarships and trade-school awards
- emergency assistance
- memorial-family support
- descendant heritage participation
- volunteer opportunities

## What makes YAT-YAS different

### Intergenerational by design

YAT-YAS should not separate the LVT, LVTP, AAV, and ACV generations. A Marine from an earlier platform and a current ACV Marine should be able to belong to the same living network and historical record.

### Digital-first, not social-media dependent

A Facebook group can support the community, but it should not be the Association's institutional memory. YATYASLegacy.com should become the permanent system of record for member-controlled profiles, history, programs, events, and governance.

### Useful while still serving

The Association should give active-duty Marines a reason to participate now through mentorship, professional networking, history, events, and transition preparation.

### History that can be searched

Oral histories, unit history, locations, people, platforms, deployments, and photographs should eventually be tagged and searchable instead of existing only as scattered posts.

### Family has a defined place

Spouses and descendants should have meaningful roles through Legacy Member programs, events, memorial projects, volunteering, scholarships, and heritage preservation while membership composition remains compliant with the Association's tax status.

### Mission before merchandise

Coins, shirts, hats, and events can support revenue and identity, but the Association should be known for programs and measurable impact rather than merchandise alone.

## Cultural guardrails

YAT-YAS should avoid becoming:

- a reunion-only organization
- a social-media-only community
- a vehicle-platform rivalry
- an organization centered only on retirees
- an organization that stores sensitive service documents in public systems
- an organization that implies official U.S. Marine Corps or DoD endorsement

## Member value proposition

The answer to "Why should I join?" should be clear:

> Membership connects you to the permanent network and historical record of the Amtracker community — the people you served with, the generations before you, the Marines coming after you, and programs that support the next mission.

## Long-term impact

Success is not measured only by the number of dues-paying members. The Association should track:

- verified members
- active-duty participation
- veterans and retirees participating
- mentorship matches
- members reconnected through Crew Finder
- oral histories preserved
- memorial profiles created
- photographs and historical records preserved
- scholarship and assistance dollars delivered
- event attendance
- chapter activity
- volunteer hours
- museum/restoration projects supported
- member voting participation
- family participation
