# YATYASLegacy.com — Digital Platform Blueprint

Status: Product and information-architecture working draft. Build in phases; do not delay legal formation waiting for the full platform.

## Product principle

YATYASLegacy.com should evolve from a public Association website into the digital headquarters and institutional memory of the Amtracker community.

The site should not be just a marketing page. Over time it should support membership, history, mentorship, events, chapters, memorials, and governance.

## Member portal

### Dashboard

Show:

- member name
- member status
- Founding Charter number, if applicable
- digital membership card
- dues status
- upcoming events
- chapter
- mentorship status
- recent Association updates
- open votes, when applicable

### My Legacy Profile

Member-controlled record containing service-history and community-history information.

Privacy controls should allow:

- private
- members only
- selected fields public

### Digital Membership Card

Potential fields:

- member name
- membership class
- member number
- Founding Charter number
- status
- expiration/renewal state
- verification status

Do not place sensitive service-document identifiers on the card.

### Crew Finder

Search or filter by approved profile fields including unit, era, platform, duty station, deployment, and schoolhouse.

### Legacy Registry

Browse member-approved historical profiles and cross-link related oral histories, units, locations, and photographs.

### Oral Histories

Searchable interviews with transcript, tags, speaker information, related locations, and related members.

### Final Splash

### Heritage Map

Interactive geographic discovery of the community's history.

## Founding 100 experience

Founding 100 members should receive:

- permanent number 001–100
- digital Founding Charter badge
- member profile designation
- certificate
- challenge coin when produced
- permanent recognition page
- Muster recognition

Founding Charter recognition is historical and non-transferable.

## Administrative portal

Authorized administrators should eventually have:

- member review queue
- membership-composition dashboard
- dues status
- Founding 100 number assignment
- event administration
- content moderation
- chapter administration
- oral-history review
- Final Splash review
- voting/election administration
- program metrics
- audit log

### Critical compliance dashboard

Display aggregate counts for:

- total legal members
- qualifying current/former Armed Forces members
- qualifying family/cadet categories
- war-veteran count where tracked
- ratio alerts before admitting a membership category that could affect Section 501(c)(19) compliance

Do not expose this data publicly at the member-identifiable level.

## Security and privacy requirements

- Never store military IDs, DD-214s, Social Security numbers, banking credentials, or tax IDs in the public repository.
- Use private encrypted storage for eligibility evidence if evidence must be retained.
- Minimize retained documents; store a verification result instead of a full document when legally and operationally appropriate.
- Use role-based access.
- Maintain audit logs for sensitive administrative actions.
- Require MFA for privileged administrators.
- Allow members to control public profile visibility.
- Obtain family approval before publishing memorial or family-submitted information.
- Establish a documented process for correction and removal of member-submitted historical content.
