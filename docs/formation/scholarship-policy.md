# YAT-YAS Association, Inc. — Scholarship Policy Draft

Status: Working draft for board adoption. Not open for applications until the board sets award amounts, dates, and an application process.

## 1. Purpose

Support education for the next generation of the amphibious tracks community — children and dependents of Alligator Marines connected to the LVT / LVTP / AAV / ACV lineage — consistent with the Association’s veterans-support and family-support purposes under IRC §501(c)(19).

Scholarships are **not** open to the general public.

## 2. Association Annual Scholarship

The Association may award one or more annual scholarships at the national level. Award count, dollar amount, and deadlines are set by board resolution each cycle and published when the program opens.

## 3. Eligibility (who can apply)

An applicant must be:

1. A child, stepchild, adopted child, or other dependent of a **tracks community sponsor** who is:
   - a past or present U.S. Armed Forces member associated with the amphibious-vehicle / Assault Amphibian community (including 2d, 3d, and 4th Tracks lineage and related LVT–ACV service); and
   - an Association member in good standing, **or** otherwise verified as eligible by the board until membership administration is fully standing; and
2. Enrolled in, or accepted to, an eligible education program (high school completion pathway, trade/vocational school, community college, or undergraduate college or university); and
3. Willing to provide verification requested by the Association (sponsor service/membership proof, dependent relationship, and school enrollment or acceptance).

Active duty, reserve, and veteran sponsors may all qualify when the amphibious tracks connection is shown.

## 4. What the award supports

Awards are scholarships paid directly to the recipient. The Association does not require payment to the school or receipt reimbursement. Recipients decide how to apply the funds toward their education or trade path. Awards are not wages or dues refunds.

## 5. Selection

A disinterested review panel or board committee evaluates complete applications under published criteria (for example need, academic or training progress, community connection, and essay or statement quality). Directors or officers with a conflict (including a family applicant) must disclose and recuse.

## 6. Chapter scholarships

Local chapters may establish their own annual scholarships after chapter formation and board acknowledgment. Chapter awards must:

- follow this policy’s eligibility spirit (tracks community dependents — not the general public);
- use written criteria and conflict-of-interest rules;
- stay within chapter budget and Association financial controls; and
- not use Association national scholarship funds without board approval.

## 7. Records and compliance

The Association keeps applications, award decisions, payment records, and conflict disclosures as required by its financial-controls policy and applicable law. This program does not create membership rights, ownership of Association assets, or a guarantee of annual funding.

## 8. Amendment

The board may amend this policy by resolution. Material changes should be posted before the next application cycle opens.
