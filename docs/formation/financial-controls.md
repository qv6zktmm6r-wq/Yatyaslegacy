# YAT-YAS Association, Inc. — Financial Controls Draft

Status: Working draft.

## 1. Purpose

These controls are intended to protect nonprofit assets, document board oversight, and reduce conflicts of interest.

## 2. Bank Accounts

All bank accounts must be held in the legal name of YAT-YAS Association, Inc.

No corporate funds may be commingled with personal funds.

Authorized signers must be approved by board resolution.

## 3. Receipts and Documentation

Every disbursement should have supporting documentation appropriate to the transaction, such as an invoice, receipt, contract, reimbursement form, or board authorization.

## 4. Reimbursements

Reimbursements are permitted only for legitimate corporation expenses supported by adequate documentation.

A person should not approve their own reimbursement.

## 5. Compensation

Compensation may be paid only for bona fide services actually rendered.

Compensation must be:

- reasonable
- documented
- approved by disinterested directors
- supported by appropriate comparability information when material
- recorded in the minutes or a written board consent

A compensated director or officer must not participate in the approval of their own compensation except to provide requested factual information.

## 6. Related-Party Transactions

Any proposed transaction involving a director, officer, key person, family member, or related business must be disclosed before approval.

The disinterested directors must determine that the transaction is fair, reasonable, and in the corporation's best interest and must document the basis for approval.

## 7. Major Expenditures

Routine budgeted expenditures below $1,000 may be approved by an authorized officer acting within the officer's delegated budget authority.

Expenditures from $1,000 through $5,000 require approval by two authorized persons. The approvers should be independent of the payee or beneficiary of the transaction whenever practicable.

Expenditures over $5,000 require approval of the full board in accordance with the bylaws and applicable quorum and voting requirements.

Any major unbudgeted commitment requires full board approval regardless of amount.

These thresholds are the approved working governance decision and become operative when formally adopted by the board at the organizational meeting or by other valid board action.

## 8. Budget

The board should approve an annual operating budget and review actual results against budget periodically.

## 9. Financial Reporting

The Treasurer should provide periodic financial reports to the board showing, at minimum:

- cash balances
- income
- expenses
- material unpaid obligations
- restricted funds, if any
- budget variance information

## 10. Founder / Officer Controls

The Founder designation does not authorize unilateral use of corporate funds.

No officer, director, or member has an ownership claim to nonprofit assets.

## 11. Record Retention

The corporation should maintain appropriate accounting records, tax filings, bank statements, receipts, contracts, minutes, and donor records in accordance with applicable law and an adopted records-retention schedule.

## 12. Public Repository Restrictions

Do not commit banking credentials, account/routing numbers, tax IDs, donor private data, member identity documents, or other sensitive financial information to GitHub.
