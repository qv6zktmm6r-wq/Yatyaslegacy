# YAT-YAS Association, Inc. — Membership Policy Draft

Status: Working draft.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the initial membership classes and administrative safeguards for maintaining YAT-YAS Association, Inc. as a qualifying veterans organization.

## 2. Voting Service/Veteran Members

A Voting Service/Veteran Member must:

- be a qualifying past or present member of the U.S. Armed Forces
- provide acceptable eligibility verification through a secure process
- remain in good standing
- comply with the bylaws and code of conduct

Each voting member has one vote.

Active-duty members may qualify through current service verification; a DD-214 is not required for a member who is still serving.

## 3. Founding Charter 001–100

The first 100 qualifying members may receive a permanent charter number from 001 through 100.

The designation:

- is permanent
- is non-transferable
- carries no ownership interest
- does not create a superior governance vote
- does not entitle the holder to corporate assets

Initial dues: $5/month or $50/year.

## 4. Regular Voting Members

Initial dues: $10/month or $100/year.

## 5. Legacy Members

Legacy Members may include family categories permitted under applicable Section 501(c)(19) rules.

Initial status: nonvoting.

## 6. Supporters

Supporters may donate, volunteer, attend eligible events, or otherwise support the mission but are not corporate members and have no governance vote unless separately admitted to a voting membership class.

## 7. Dues Changes

Any change in dues should be approved by the board and communicated to affected members at least 30 days before the change becomes effective.

## 8. Membership Composition Compliance

The corporation shall maintain administrative counters sufficient to monitor:

- total members
- past or present Armed Forces members
- qualifying cadet categories, if any
- qualifying spouses, surviving spouses, ancestors, and lineal descendants
- war veterans where relevant to the applicable federal tests

Current federal public guidance and recent IRS enforcement materials are not perfectly aligned on the war-veteran percentage applicable to exemption. Until the corporation receives its determination or qualified tax advice resolving the issue, membership administration should conservatively track both the general 75% service-member test and the 75% war-veteran test described in Treasury Regulation 1.501(c)(19)-1, plus the separate 90% war-veteran rule relevant to charitable-contribution deductibility.

## 9. Sensitive Records

Eligibility records must be stored securely outside the public Git repository.

Do not commit DD-214s, military IDs, dates of birth, Social Security numbers, VA records, or other identity documents to GitHub.

The repository may store only aggregate membership-composition counts and non-sensitive policy documentation.

## 10. Suspension and Termination

Membership may be suspended or terminated for loss of eligibility, nonpayment of dues after applicable notice, material violation of governing documents, fraud, or serious misconduct, subject to fair procedures required by law and the bylaws.

## 11. Membership Register

The corporation shall maintain an official membership register containing at least the information required for governance and tax-compliance administration.
