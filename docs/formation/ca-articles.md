## Article IV — Purpose

This corporation is a nonprofit public benefit corporation and is not organized for the private gain of any person. It is organized under the Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation Law for public purposes.

The specific purpose of this corporation is to preserve and perpetuate the history, traditions, fellowship, welfare, and legacy of past and present members of the United States Armed Forces associated with the amphibious-vehicle community, including the LVT, LVTP, AAV, and ACV communities, and to conduct veterans-support, historical, educational, patriotic, memorial, mentorship, scholarship, family-support, fellowship, social, recreational, museum, restoration, oral-history, reunion, and related programs consistent with war veteran purposes within the meaning of Internal Revenue Code Section 501(c)(19).

## Article V — Additional Statements for Exempt Status

This corporation is organized and operated exclusively for war veteran purposes within the meaning of Internal Revenue Code Section 501(c)(19).

The property of this corporation is irrevocably dedicated to war veteran purposes and no part of the net income or assets of this corporation shall ever inure to the benefit of any director, officer, or member thereof or to the benefit of any private person.

Upon the dissolution or winding up of the corporation, all assets remaining after payment or provision for payment of all debts and liabilities of this corporation shall be distributed to a nonprofit fund, foundation, or corporation which is organized and operated exclusively for war veteran purposes and which has established its tax-exempt status under Internal Revenue Code Section 501(c)(19).
