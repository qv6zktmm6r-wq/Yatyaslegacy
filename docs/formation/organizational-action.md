### 4. Founder and Co-Founder Designations; Protected Seats

### 5. Founding Board

RESOLVED, that the corporation authorizes a seven-seat Founding Board of Directors.

The currently identified initial directors are:

1. Jorge A. Mejia Lozoya — Protected Founder-Director Seat  
2. Jose Guerra — Protected Co-Founder-Director Seat  
3. Fausto Cabrera — Director  
4–7. Open — may be filled by the directors then in office as permitted by California law, after each appointee affirmatively consents to serve

Once all seven seats are filled, four directors constitute a quorum. While vacancies exist, quorum shall be determined under the bylaws.

After the member-election system is established, expiring director seats other than the Protected Founder-Director Seat and Protected Co-Founder-Director Seat shall be elected by Voting Service/Veteran Members in good standing under a Board-approved election policy.

### 6. Fiscal Year

RESOLVED, that the fiscal year of the corporation shall run from October 1 through September 30.

### 7. EIN

RESOLVED, that the President (or another authorized officer) is directed to obtain an Employer Identification Number from the Internal Revenue Service using the data in `docs/formation/ein-data-sheet.md`, and to store the EIN confirmation securely outside the public Git repository.

### 8. Statement of Information

RESOLVED, that an authorized officer is directed to file the initial California Statement of Information by the bizfile due date of **February 2, 2027**, using `docs/formation/si-100-data-sheet.md`.

### 9. Bank Account and Signers

RESOLVED, that the corporation may open one or more deposit accounts in its legal name after receipt of the EIN and required formation documents, and that the separate bank resolution in `docs/formation/bank-resolution.md` is approved in substantially the form presented.

Proposed initial authorized signers (subject to bank forms):

- Jorge A. Mejia Lozoya — President  
- Victoria Guerra — Treasurer  

### 10. Membership Structure

RESOLVED, that the initial membership structure is approved in substantially the form contained in `docs/formation/membership-policy.md`, subject to compliance with Section 501(c)(19) membership-composition requirements.

### 11. Membership Pricing

RESOLVED, that the first 100 approved qualifying members may receive the permanent **First Wave** designation numbered FW-001 through FW-100.

First Wave entrance pricing and welcome-kit pricing remain pending final Board approval.

Regular Voting Service/Veteran Member dues also remain subject to final Board approval before collection.

Any future dues change shall follow the notice requirements in the membership policy.

### 12. Conflict, Compensation, and Financial Controls

RESOLVED, that compensation may be paid only for bona fide services or documented reimbursement, must be reasonable, and must be approved by disinterested directors; and that the financial-controls policy in `docs/formation/financial-controls.md` is adopted.

### 13. Major Expenditure Threshold

RESOLVED, that routine budgeted expenditures below $1,000 may be approved by an authorized officer within delegated authority; expenditures from $1,000 through $5,000 require two-person approval; expenditures over $5,000 require full Board approval; and any major unbudgeted commitment requires full Board approval regardless of amount.

### 14. Tax-Exemption Filings

RESOLVED, that authorized officers are directed to prepare and submit the federal Form 1024 / Schedule K path and California tax-exemption filings after formation and EIN issuance.

### 15. Records

RESOLVED, that the Secretary or, until a Secretary is appointed, the President shall maintain the Articles, bylaws, minutes, resolutions, tax filings, membership-composition records, and other required corporate records.
