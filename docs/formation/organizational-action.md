## 5. Founding Board

RESOLVED, that the corporation authorizes a seven-seat Founding Board of Directors.

The currently identified initial directors are:

- Jorge A. Mejia Lozoya
- Jose Guerra
- Fausto Cabrera

Seats four through seven are initially open and may be filled by the directors then in office as permitted by California law, after each appointee affirmatively consents to serve.

Once all seven seats are filled, four directors constitute a quorum. While vacancies exist, quorum shall be determined under the bylaws.

After the member-election system is established, expiring director seats other than the Protected Founder-Director Seat and Protected Co-Founder-Director Seat shall be elected by Voting Service/Veteran Members in good standing under a Board-approved election policy.

## 11. Membership Pricing

RESOLVED, that the first 100 approved qualifying members may receive the permanent **First Wave** designation numbered FW-001 through FW-100.

## 12. Conflict and Compensation Controls

RESOLVED, that compensation may be paid only for bona fide services or documented reimbursement, must be reasonable, and must be approved by disinterested directors in accordance with applicable law and the corporation's financial-controls policy.

## 13. Major Expenditure Threshold

RESOLVED, that routine budgeted expenditures below $1,000 may be approved by an authorized officer within delegated authority; expenditures from $1,000 through $5,000 require two-person approval; expenditures over $5,000 require full board approval; and any major unbudgeted commitment requires full board approval regardless of amount.

## 14. Tax-Exemption Filings

RESOLVED, that authorized officers are directed to prepare and submit the federal and California tax-exemption filings after formation and EIN issuance.

## 15. Records

RESOLVED, that the Secretary or other authorized officer shall maintain the Articles, bylaws, minutes, resolutions, tax filings, membership-composition records, and other required corporate records.
