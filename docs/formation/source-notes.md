# Formation Source Notes

Reviewed October 25, 2026.

These are the authoritative sources used to build the formation package.

## California Franchise Tax Board

FTB Form 3500 Instructions / Sample H — War Veterans under R&TC Section 23701w  
https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/misc/3500-booklet.html

Key point: Sample H supplies the nonprofit public benefit, 501(c)(19), asset-dedication, and dissolution framework for a California war-veterans corporation.

## California Secretary of State

Business entity filing information and fees  
https://www.sos.ca.gov/business-programs/business-entities

Statement of Information filing tips  
https://www.sos.ca.gov/business-programs/business-entities/statements

Key points:
- Nonprofit corporation formation filing fee: $30.
- Initial nonprofit Statement of Information: due within 90 days.
- Nonprofit Statement of Information fee: $20.
- Corporate minutes and bylaws are maintained internally and are not filed as ordinary governance records with the Secretary of State.

## Internal Revenue Service

Veterans' organizations  
https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/other-non-profits/veterans-organizations

Form 1024  
https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1024

Form 1024 Instructions  
https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1024

2026 IRS determination letter applying Treasury Regulation 1.501(c)(19)-1  
https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-wd/202611011.pdf

Key compliance note:
- IRS public guidance states at least 75% of members must be past or present Armed Forces members and at least 97.5% must be within the permitted service/cadet/family categories.
- The 2026 determination letter applies the regulation as requiring at least 75% war veterans.
- Contributions have a separate 90% war-veteran membership rule for federal charitable-contribution deductibility.
- Until YAT-YAS has a determination or qualified tax advice resolving the discrepancy, membership administration should conservatively track all relevant thresholds.

## California Attorney General — Registry of Charities and Fundraisers

Initial Registration  
https://oag.ca.gov/charities/initial-reg

Key point: a charitable organization subject to registration generally must register within 30 days after first receiving charitable assets. The current initial registration fee is $50.

## Repository privacy

This repository is public. Do not commit:
- DD-214s
- military IDs
- dates of birth
- Social Security numbers
- EIN confirmation letters containing sensitive data
- bank account/routing data
- member identity documents
- private addresses not already intended for public filing
