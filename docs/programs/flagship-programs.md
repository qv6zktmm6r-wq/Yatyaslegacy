# YAT-YAS Association — Flagship Programs

Status: Working program architecture. Program launch, funding, eligibility, and operating policies require Board approval as appropriate.

## 1. YAT-YAS Legacy Registry

A member-controlled historical registry documenting the people who built and continue the amphibious-vehicle community.

Potential profile fields:

- name
- service era
- unit history
- duty stations
- platform history: LVT / LVTP / AAV / ACV
- billets
- deployments
- schools
- photographs
- awards or notable service, when voluntarily provided
- personal stories
- current professional field
- mentorship availability

### Privacy principle

Members control what is publicly visible. Service-verification records must not be publicly displayed and should not be stored in this public repository.

## 2. Crew Finder

A private member feature that helps members reconnect using structured filters such as:

- unit
- year range
- duty station
- platform
- deployment
- schoolhouse
- billet

The goal is to answer questions like:

> Who else was at 3d Tracks during my years there?

Crew Finder should never expose sensitive personal information without member consent.

## 3. Track to Next Mission

A mentorship and transition program connecting experienced Amtrackers with active-duty Marines and transitioning veterans.

Potential tracks:

- civilian careers
- federal careers
- education
- trade schools
- entrepreneurship
- leadership
- family transition
- financial-readiness resources
- veteran-service navigation through qualified partner organizations

The program should facilitate connections and education, not represent itself as providing legal, financial, or medical professional services unless appropriately qualified.

## 4. Amtracker Oral History Project

Capture first-hand stories across generations before they are lost.

Each interview may capture:

- service period
- units
- MOS or role
- platform
- deployments and operations
- memorable leaders and crew members
- major changes in the community
- lessons learned
- photographs and artifacts

Long-term digital capabilities may include:

- transcription
- searchable transcripts
- topic tags
- unit tags
- platform tags
- location tags
- cross-links to the Legacy Registry and Heritage Map

AI may assist with transcription, indexing, tagging, and discovery, but historical claims should remain traceable to source material.

## 5. Final Splash

A permanent memorial program recognizing deceased members of the Amtracker community.

Each memorial profile may include, with family permission:

- name
- photograph
- service dates
- unit history
- platform history
- biography
- family-provided remembrance
- memorial or funeral information when appropriate
- oral-history excerpts
- links to related historical records

The annual YAT-YAS Muster may include recognition of members added to Final Splash during the prior year.

## 6. YAT-YAS Heritage Map

An interactive map connecting history to place.

Potential layers:

- duty stations
- historical units
- training locations
- amphibious operations
- deployments
- museums
- memorials
- restoration projects
- member-submitted stories
- oral histories tied to locations

The map should use reviewed historical data and clearly distinguish verified records from member recollections.

## 7. YAT-YAS Muster

The annual flagship gathering.

Potential components:

- Association business meeting
- reunion activities
- Final Splash remembrance
- heritage presentations
- oral-history recording station
- mentorship and career sessions
- family activities
- museum/restoration partners
- recognition and awards
- scholarship presentations
- Founding 100 recognition
- chapter leadership sessions

## 8. YAT-YAS Breakfast Clubs

Simple local gatherings designed to keep the community connected between national events.

Operating principle:

> No ceremony required. Just Amtrackers at a table.

Breakfast Clubs can serve as the lowest-friction path toward future chapters without requiring every gathering to become a formal chapter immediately.

## 9. Chapters

Regional chapters may support:

- breakfasts
- family events
- volunteer projects
- memorial activities
- oral histories
- museum support
- mentorship
- local fundraising authorized under national policy

Chapter financial authority, branding, governance, and use of the Association's EIN must remain controlled by national policy and Board authorization.

## 10. Emergency Brotherhood Fund

A future restricted or board-designated assistance program for legitimate member or family hardship.

Before launch, the Board should adopt written eligibility, review, conflict, documentation, and disbursement procedures so assistance is mission-aligned, consistent, and not controlled by a single person.

Potential uses:

- emergency travel
- funeral-related support
- short-term family hardship
- disaster-related assistance
- other qualifying needs approved under policy

## 11. Scholarships and Trade-School Awards

Future awards may support members or qualifying family participants pursuing:

- college
- trade school
- certifications
- apprenticeships
- professional education

The Board should adopt objective eligibility and selection criteria before accepting restricted scholarship donations.

## 12. Museum and Restoration Support

YAT-YAS may support qualifying projects involving:

- museums
- historical displays
- vehicle restoration
- artifact preservation
- digitization
- archival work
- educational exhibits

Support should be documented and tied to the Association's exempt purposes.

## 13. Family and Legacy Program

A structured place for spouses, descendants, and families to participate.

Potential activities:

- family history submissions
- heritage events
- volunteer projects
- memorial support
- scholarships
- family activities at the Muster
- spouse-led operational roles
- descendant history projects

Legal membership classifications remain governed by the bylaws and Section 501(c)(19) compliance rules.
